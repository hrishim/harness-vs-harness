Suggestions for improvement

These are general habits, not fixes for one test.

  1. Plan before working. Pi wrote a visible plan in only 3 of its 90 runs. In the other three harnesses, runs that wrote a plan first passed more often (92% of 36 runs against 84% of 185), at a higher cost (a median of 128K real tokens against 82K). A short plan listing the inputs, the tools available, the method and a checklist could help. This rests on few runs, so treat it as a lead and not as proof.
  2. Check what tools are installed before choosing a method. In the Expense claim, the round that never looked for OCR read 19 photos by eye and missed one. The rounds that used OCR passed.
  3. Look up a library's functions before calling them. All eight failed calls in the Expense claim were scripts calling pypdf functions that do not exist. Checking the library's own help first would have avoided the retries.
  4. Check the result against every input at the end. Round 1 never mentioned the breakfast receipt it missed. A final pass over every input would have caught it.
